The **best Toyota 4Runner years to buy** in 2026 are usually **1999-2002** if you want an older durable classic, **2006-2009** if you want the best fourth-generation lane, and **2014-2022** if you want the safest modern used 4Runner stretch.

That is the short answer. The longer answer is that the 4Runner is one of the most trustworthy used SUVs you can buy, but buyers still need to separate strong generations from overpriced nostalgia and rusty trucks living off reputation.

## Quick answer: best 4Runner years to buy

- **Best older years:** **1999-2002**
- **Best mid-era years:** **2006-2009**
- **Best newer years:** **2014-2022**
- **Years that need more caution:** **2003-2005**, any heavily rusted older truck, and the newest redesign years until more long-mileage data builds

## Best Toyota 4Runner years to buy

### 1. 2014-2022 4Runner: safest modern used-buy lane

For most buyers, this is the easiest recommendation.

Why these years stand out:
- fifth generation was mature by this point
- the **4.0L V6** is proven and widely trusted
- you get better everyday livability without leaving the proven 4Runner formula
- these years still have serious resale, but they carry the lowest surprise-risk among newer used 4Runners

If you want to buy once and keep it a long time, this is usually the cleanest lane.

### 2. 2006-2009 4Runner: best fourth-generation years

These years are strong if you want a slightly older, better-value 4Runner.

Why they work:
- fourth gen had already settled in by then
- fewer early-platform bugs than the first few years of that generation
- V8 and V6 options both have loyal long-term followings

These can be great values if the SUV is clean and rust-free.

### 3. 1999-2002 4Runner: the older durability favorite

These are still loved for a reason.

Why buyers chase them:
- simple, proven design
- strong long-term durability track record
- high respect among people who care about long ownership, not just trendy tech

Why to stay disciplined:
- age itself now matters a lot
- rust can override every other good thing about the vehicle
- clean examples are getting rarer and often priced accordingly

## 4Runner years that need more caution

### 2003-2005 4Runner

These are not automatic avoids, but they deserve more scrutiny.

Why:
- earlier fourth-generation years carry more first-wave platform risk
- age and underbody wear are now real factors
- sellers still often price them like all 4Runners are equally safe bets

### Any older rust-belt 4Runner

This matters more than people want to admit.

A strong drivetrain does not cancel:
- bad frame corrosion
- underbody hardware rot
- suspension mounting-point rust

A rust-free older 4Runner can be a gem. A crusty one is how buyers end up paying a Toyota premium for a future headache.

### Newest redesign years

These may turn out great, but they do not yet have the same high-mileage proof as the mature fifth-generation lane. If your whole reason for buying a 4Runner is durability confidence, the mature years still win.

## What matters more than model year on a used 4Runner

Model year matters, but after six figures on the odometer these matter more:

1. **frame and underbody condition**
2. **maintenance history**
3. **cooling-system and fluid-service discipline**
4. **suspension wear**, especially if the SUV was used off-road
5. **whether the seller is pricing based on condition or just 4Runner hype**

## What to inspect before buying a used 4Runner

Before paying the 4Runner premium, check:

- **frame and rust condition**
- **cooling-system service**
- **suspension and steering wear**
- **evidence of hard off-road use**
- **fluid-change history**
- **whether the SUV feels tight and honest on the road**

A clean service-record 4Runner at higher mileage is usually a better buy than a lower-mileage one with a mystery past.
